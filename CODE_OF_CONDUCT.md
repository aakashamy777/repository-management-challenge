# Mergington High School Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior: being respectful, welcoming, and constructive.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, insults, and personal attacks.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking fair action on unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the IT Club faculty advisor. All reports will be reviewed and handled fairly.
