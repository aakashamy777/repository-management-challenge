# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

1. Do not open a public issue or discuss the problem publicly.
2. Use the repository's Security tab and click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Complete the form with as much detail as you can.
4. Email the IT Club faculty advisor to say a report was submitted, without including vulnerability details.

## Response Timeline

- We will acknowledge your report within 2 school days.
- We will share an initial assessment within 5 school days.
- Critical issues involving student data get immediate action.
- We may use a private fork and invite you as a collaborator.

## Thank You

Thank you for practicing responsible disclosure and helping keep our students safe.
